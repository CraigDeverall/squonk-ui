# Accordion

<ResizableCanvas of={AccordionStories.Default} defaultHeight={300} />

`Accordion` stacks sections that open and close under their titles. It suits
content people scan for the one part they need (frequently asked questions,
detail most people skip, sections of a review page), so the page stays short.

```tsx
import { Accordion } from "@squonk/ui/accordion";

<Accordion
  items={[
    { value: "due", title: "When are my annual dues due?", content: <DueDates /> },
    { value: "late", title: "What happens if I pay late?", content: <LateDues /> },
  ]}
/>
```

Don't hide content most people need: if everyone opens a section, show it.
Use `Tabs` to switch between parallel views, and a `Modal` or `Drawer` for a task.

## Contained

`variant="contained"` draws a bordered box around the sections, for an accordion
that sits in a page among other content.

<ResizableCanvas of={AccordionStories.Contained} defaultHeight={300} />

## Rows that shade on hover

`hover="fill"` shades the whole header row while it is pointed at, and leaves
the title without an underline. Use it when the sections read as rows of a list,
such as messages or records that open for their details. `hover="underline"`
underlines the title only, even in a contained accordion.

In a divided accordion the shading reaches a little past the text at both sides,
so the text stays in line with the content around it. Leave room for it: put the
accordion inside padding, such as a card's. With `hover="fill"` you may also set
`indicator={false}` to drop the chevron, when the rows plainly open.

<ResizableCanvas of={AccordionStories.FillHover} defaultHeight={300} />

## Several open at once

By default opening one section closes the other. Set `multiple` when people
compare sections or work through several.

## Summaries

`description` adds a line under the title: a summary, a status or what's inside,
so people can tell whether to open it.

## Accessibility

- The WAI-ARIA accordion pattern: each title is a `button` inside a heading, with
  `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; each open section is a `region` labelled by
  its title.
- Set `headingLevel` so the titles fit the page's heading outline (3 by default).
- Tab moves through the titles and into open content. Arrow keys also move
  between titles, Home and End to the ends; Enter or Space toggles.
- Opening and closing animate the height, and don't animate when people ask for
  reduced motion.

## Common pitfalls

- **Don't put required form fields in a closed section.** People miss them,
  and errors inside closed sections are invisible.
- **Don't nest accordions.**
- **Don't make titles vague.** "More information" five times tells people nothing;
  each title should say what is inside.
