# Components

Every component in SquonkUI, grouped by the job it does. Each example below
is the real component, in the current theme: try them, switch the theme in the
toolbar, then follow a name to its page for variants, accessibility notes and code.

## How the pieces fit

- **Components own their behaviour, not their placement.** A `Modal` traps focus
  and closes on `Escape`; a `TextField` carries its label, help and error. None of
  them takes a margin. Where things go is decided by the layout components around
  them.
- **Only stacks and grids take layout props.** `gap`, `justify`, `padding` and
  `columns` live on `VStack`, `HStack`, `Grid`, `Block` and friends, with one
  vocabulary everywhere (`gap="md"` means the same in each). Named layouts such as
  `Card` and `Section` take intent instead (`variant`, `tone`), and follow the
  surrounding register.
- **Props say what you mean.** `variant="primary"`, `tone="error"`. The theme
  decides how that looks, in light and dark mode.
- **Text follows the register.** Wrap a dense panel in `Presentation register="compact"`
  and its buttons, fields and text tighten together.
- **A new layout earns its name.** Extract one when an arrangement has a name in
  product language, appears in three or more places, and needs a narrower API
  than the stacks it's built from. Otherwise keep it as stacks in that screen.

## Choosing between similar components

- **Button or Link?** It changes data or state: `Button`. It changes the URL: `Link`.
- **Checkbox or Switch?** Saved with a form: `Checkbox`. Applies straight away: `Switch`.
- **Badge or Tag?** Reports a status or count: `Badge`. Names a value someone chose: `Tag`.
  See [Labels: Badge or Tag](?path=/docs/patterns-labels-badge-or-tag--docs).
- **Alert, Toast or Modal?** In context: `Alert`. A brief confirmation: `Toast`. Needs an
  answer before continuing: `Modal`. See [Toasts, alerts and dialogs](?path=/docs/patterns-toasts-alerts-and-dialogs--docs).
- **Spinner, Progress Bar or Skeleton?** You know the content's shape: `Skeleton`. You can
  measure progress: `Progress Bar`. A short wait: `Spinner`.
