# Printable, NotPrintable and PrintOnly

<ResizableCanvas of={PrintableStories.Statement} />

Three marks that decide what goes on paper. None of them adds a box or changes
anything on screen. Print the example above (Ctrl+P or ⌘P) to see them work:
only the statement prints, the app bar and help line are left off, and a
letterhead and reference appear.

```tsx
import { NotPrintable, Printable, PrintOnly } from "@squonkstack/ui/printable";

<NotPrintable><AppHeader /></NotPrintable>
<Printable>
  <PrintOnly><Letterhead /></PrintOnly>
  <Heading level={1}>Statement of account</Heading>
  <Table … />
</Printable>
```

- **`Printable`** marks what to print. While at least one is on the page, printing
  includes only the `Printable` areas and everything inside them. The layout
  around them is flattened, so a statement deep in a sidebar layout starts at
  the top of the paper. With none on the page, the whole page prints. Areas can
  sit anywhere on the page and can nest.
- **`NotPrintable`** leaves its contents off paper, anywhere, including inside a
  `Printable`: actions, navigation, filters, help that only makes sense on
  screen.
- **`PrintOnly`** shows its contents only on paper: a letterhead, a reference
  number, the date printed, a note that stands in for an interactive control.

## Props

`Printable` takes `breakBefore` (start the area on a new page) and
`keepTogether` (keep it on one page where it fits, rather than splitting it).
All three take `inline`, which renders a `span` instead of a `div` for a mark
inside a line of text, and pass other attributes (an `id`, a `data-testid`) to
the element.

## What every component does on paper

You don't need these marks for a page to print well. Whenever a page prints,
the library restyles it from the theme's print purposes:

- Colours become ink on white, so a dark theme doesn't print a black page. A
  theme that should keep its colours turns on the `printBackgrounds` switch.
- Shadows, relief and motion are switched off.
- Buttons are left off: they do nothing on paper. So are pagination, toasts and
  tooltips.
- Links to web and email addresses print their address after the text. A
  theme turns that off with the `printLinkUrls` switch.
- Tables print every row, repeat their header on each page and don't split a
  row across pages. Scroll boxes print all of their contents.
- Cards are kept on one page where they fit, and pinned headers print once, in
  place.

See [Printing](?path=/docs/patterns-printing--docs) for when to use each mark.

## Accessibility

`PrintOnly` is hidden on screen from everyone, including screen reader users, so
put nothing there that people on screen need. `NotPrintable` and `Printable`
change nothing on screen.

## Pitfalls

- **Don't hide what a printed record needs.** A printed statement still needs
  its totals, dates and reference, even when they sit in a part of the screen
  you'd otherwise leave off.
- **Wrap whole regions.** Marking the content you want, once, is sturdier than
  marking everything else `NotPrintable`.
- **A `PrintOnly` outside every `Printable` doesn't print** while a `Printable`
  is on the page, like anything else outside one.
