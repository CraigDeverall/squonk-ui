# Accessibility

Every interactive component is built on [React Aria](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/),
which implements the WAI-ARIA patterns and is tested across browsers and screen
readers. That covers the mechanics. Whether a screen is accessible still depends
on what you put in it.

## What every component gives you

- **Names and descriptions wired up.** A field's label, help and error are linked
  to its input, so they are read together.
- **Keyboard support** for each pattern: arrow keys in menus, lists, tabs and
  grids; Escape to close overlays; Space and Enter where expected.
- **Visible focus** only for keyboard users, with a focus ring that meets contrast.
- **Focus management** in overlays: focus moves into a dialog, stays there, and
  returns to the trigger when it closes.
- **Announcements** for changes people can't see, such as sort order, selection
  and notifications.
- **Reduced motion** respected, and controls at least 24px tall even in the compact register.

## What your app still has to do

- **Write real labels.** Every field has a visible label; every icon-only button
  has a `label`. A placeholder is not a label.
- **Structure the page.** One `h1`, headings in order, landmarks from `Page`,
  `Header` and `Sidebar`.
- **Write errors people can act on.** "Enter your email address", not "Invalid".
- **Keep a sensible focus order** when you compose components, and move focus on
  purpose after big changes, such as to the error summary after a failed submit.
- **Check custom colours.** The library tests its own text and surfaces; brand
  overrides, text on images and translucent panels are yours to check (4.5:1 for
  text, 3:1 for large text and control edges).
- **Don't use colour alone** to show status, selection or errors.

## Testing

Use the keyboard alone through each task, zoom to 200%, try a phone width, and run
a screen reader (VoiceOver or NVDA) over new screens. Automated checks such as axe
catch missing names and contrast, but not whether the experience makes sense. See
[Focus management](?path=/docs/patterns-focus-management--docs) and
[Labels, help and validation](?path=/docs/patterns-labels-help-and-validation--docs).
