# Colour

Colour carries meaning, so it is used sparingly: a calm neutral page, Squonk
blue for the thing to act on, and status colours only when something has a
status. Every swatch below is read from the theme you are viewing; switch light
and dark in the toolbar, or change a colour in the Theme panel, and they follow.

highlights. Links use the link colour, which is tuned to read as body text on
every background.

## Status

Status colours always come with words: a colour alone must never be the only way
to tell an error, a warning or success. Use them through the `tone` of a
[Badge](?path=/docs/functional-badge--docs) or an
[Alert](?path=/docs/functional-alert--docs), and say the status in their text.

<Specimen>
  <VStack gap="md">
    <HStack gap="sm" wrap>
      <Badge tone="neutral">Draft</Badge>
      <Badge tone="info">Processing</Badge>
      <Badge tone="success">Paid</Badge>
      <Badge tone="warning">Due soon</Badge>
      <Badge tone="error">Overdue</Badge>
    </HStack>
    <Alert tone="success" title="Sighting logged">We've received your sighting report for 12 September.</Alert>
    <Alert tone="error" title="Payment failed">Your bank declined the payment. Try another account.</Alert>
  </VStack>
</Specimen>

## Light and dark

Every colour has a light and a dark value; components pick the right one. A
section can take the opposite mode with `ThemeStyleBlock invertMode`, and text on
it stays readable.

<Specimen label="The same components inside ThemeStyleBlock invertMode.">
  <ThemeStyleBlock invertMode>
    <Block background="default" padding="md" radius="md">
    <VStack gap="sm">
      <Alert tone="info" title="Inverted section">Components recompute their colours for the opposite mode.</Alert>
      <HStack gap="sm" wrap>
        <Button variant="primary">Continue</Button>
        <Badge tone="warning">Due soon</Badge>
      </HStack>
    </VStack>
    </Block>
  </ThemeStyleBlock>
</Specimen>

## Checking your own colours

The library tests contrast for its own text roles on its own surfaces. Custom
colours, brand overrides, translucent panels and text over images are yours to
check: aim for 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and the edges of
controls. Change colours in the theme, not in component CSS; see
[Theme](?path=/docs/foundations-theme--docs) and
[Purpose tokens](?path=/docs/foundations-purpose-tokens--docs).
