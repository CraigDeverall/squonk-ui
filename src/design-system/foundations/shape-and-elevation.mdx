# Shape and elevation

Corners and shadows are named by role, so every control shares one shape and
every floating panel one depth. A theme changes a role once, for example
`roles.radius.control` to make every control a pill, and it reaches all of them.

Buttons default to the `pill` shape; use `shape="rounded"` to match fields in
dense forms. Keep one button shape within a task.

### Nested corners

When a rounded shape sits inside another, their curves run parallel: the inner
radius is the outer radius less the gap between them. The library does this
for segments in enclosed tabs and small buttons inside a field or tag, so a
round theme gets round buttons and a square theme square ones. It never goes
below the inner shape's own radius. Do the same in your own nested surfaces:

```css
border-radius: max(var(--squonk-roles-radius-item), calc(var(--squonk-roles-radius-surface) - var(--squonk-roles-space-surface-inset)));
```

Menus and listboxes have the pair worked out already. `radius.listItem` is
`radius.list` less the panel's inset and border. A row can't be rounder than
half its height, so `radius.list` is the surface radius limited to half a row
plus that inset and border: however round the theme, the first and last rows
follow the panel's curve instead of being cut by it. Both follow the surface
radius, the list's spacing and its text size when a theme re-points them. Use
the pair for your own panel of rows:

```css
.menu { padding: var(--squonk-roles-space-list-inset); border-radius: var(--squonk-roles-radius-list); }
.row {
  padding: var(--squonk-roles-space-list-item-inset-block) var(--squonk-roles-space-list-item-inset-inline);
  font-size: var(--squonk-roles-type-list-item);
  line-height: var(--squonk-roles-type-tight-line-height);
  border-radius: var(--squonk-roles-radius-list-item);
}
```

## Elevation

Shadows say how far something sits above the page. Most content sits flat on the
page or in an outlined card; shadows are for things that float.

## Light and relief

A theme can give surfaces a little relief: a subtle gradient across each one and
a highlight and shade along its edges, so controls look raised off the page and
fields sit slightly into it. It is off by default, and most themes should leave
it off or keep it gentle.

Everything follows one light source. `lightAngle` says where the light comes
from, in degrees clockwise from above, and turns every relief gradient, every
edge and every computed shadow above with it, so the whole interface agrees.
Shadows you write yourself in a role binding (such as a hard `3px 3px 0`) are
used as written, so draw them to match your angle.

| Primitive | Range | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reliefDepth` | 0–1 (default 0) | How far a surface's tone shifts across its face, lit side to far side. |
| `reliefSheen` | 0–1 (default 0) | The strength of the highlight and shade strokes along its edges. |
| `reliefProfile` | `soft`, `bevel`, `glass`, `pillow`, `print` | How the light falls: a smooth gradient, crisp edges, a glossy band, a cushion, or hard embossed edges. |
| `reliefBlend` | `normal`, `soft-light`, `overlay` | How the relief combines with the colour beneath. `soft-light` keeps saturated buttons cleaner. |
| `lightAngle` | 0–359 (default 0) | Where the light comes from. Also turns the drop shadows. |

### Postures

Components don't read the relief directly. Each takes the purpose tokens for its
posture, `roles.relief.<posture>.fill` (a `background-image` over its own colour)
and `.edge` (inset shadows), so a theme can change one posture without the others.

| Posture | Components | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `control` | Buttons (not ghost), select triggers, switch thumbs, the selected segment of enclosed tabs | `relief.raised` |
| `surface` | Elevated, outlined and featured cards | `relief.raisedSubtle`, half the strength |
| `chrome` | The header | `relief.raisedSubtle` |
| `well` | Text fields, text areas, searchable selects, currency fields, switch and segmented tracks, filled cards | `relief.recessed` |
| `pressed` | A button while pressed, a select while pressed or open | `relief.recessed` |

Disabled controls and ghost buttons stay flat. Re-point a posture at a
per-profile token to give it a profile of its own: here controls are cushions
and fields have crisp bevels, under one light from the upper left.

Relief is decoration: it never carries meaning, and text must stay readable at
the darkest and lightest points of a gradient. Keep depth low on large surfaces,
check dark mode (highlights are toned down there automatically) and keep the
light above the horizon: light from below reads as wrong.
