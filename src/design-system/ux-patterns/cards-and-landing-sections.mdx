# Cards and landing sections

A card is a surface that groups one thing. Whether the whole card is clickable
depends on how many things people can do with it.

## A card that goes somewhere

Use [InteractiveCard](?path=/docs/layout-interactive-card--docs) when the whole
card has one destination or one action: `as="a"` with an `href` to navigate, or
`onClick` for an action. Its heading names it. A plain
[Card](?path=/docs/layout-card--docs) is just a surface.

Keep the focus ring visible and test long headings. A pointer cursor or hover
lift doesn't make something interactive; the element underneath must be a link
or a button.

## Comparing plans

Compose [Section](?path=/docs/layout-section--docs),
[SectionHeading](?path=/docs/layout-section-heading--docs),
[Grid](?path=/docs/layout-primitives-grid--docs) and Cards. Put the same
information in the same order in every card, feature one option at most, and
compare with words, not colour alone. The cards stack in reading order on a phone.

<Example caption="One plan featured, with a badge that says why. Every plan lists price, audience and features in the same order.">
  <PricingExample />
</Example>

Your app owns prices, billing periods and the purchase flow; when the
billing period changes, every figure must change with it.
