# Menus and commands

A [DropdownMenu](?path=/docs/functional-dropdown-menu--docs) holds a short list
of things to **do** with an item. It has no value of its own and closes after a
choice. Your app owns what each command does, and any confirmation after it.

  <RowActionsExample />
</Example>

## The rules

- **Name the trigger for the item**: "Actions for statement 1042". An icon-only
  trigger still needs a name; `triggerLabel` gives it one.
- **Use verbs**: "Send reminder", not "Reminder".
- **Group related commands, and put destructive ones last**, after a separator,
  with `tone: "danger"`. Confirm or offer undo as on
  [Destructive actions](?path=/docs/patterns-destructive-actions--docs).
- **Keep it short.** A long searchable list is a [Select](?path=/docs/functional-select--docs)
  with `searchable`; places to go are [links](?path=/docs/patterns-app-navigation--docs).
