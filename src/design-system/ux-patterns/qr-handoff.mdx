# QR handoff

Use [QrCode](?path=/docs/functional-qrcode--docs) to move someone from a screen
to their phone. Say where it goes, and always offer another way in.

<Example caption="The code's purpose is stated, and people who can't scan get a link.">

</Example>

## The rules

- **Say what happens when they scan** before they reach for their phone.
- **Offer another route**: a link to send, or a short code to type. Scanning
  isn't possible for everyone.
- **Never make scanning the only way** to complete a form or read essential text.
- **Keep the contrast and the quiet border** around the code, and test the real
  encoded value at the real size on a phone; a screenshot doesn't prove it scans.

Your app owns the link: that it's valid, that it expires, that it carries no
secrets, and what happens after the scan. A scanned code doesn't mean the task
is done or the destination is safe.
