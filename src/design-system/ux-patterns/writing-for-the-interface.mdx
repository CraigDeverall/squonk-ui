# Writing for the interface

The words are part of the design. Components make a screen accessible; only the
words make it clear. Write plainly, say what will happen, and use the same word
for the same thing everywhere. This page covers the wording (microcopy) you pass
to components: labels, buttons, messages, empty states and headings.

## The basics

- **Plain words, short sentences.** Write for someone busy and reading on a
  phone. Use the words your users use, not internal names.
- **Sentence case** for everything: headings, labels, buttons, menu items
  ("Add a bank account", not "Add A Bank Account").
- **Say "you"** for the reader. Use "we" for the organisation, sparingly.
- **Front-load.** Put the important words first, so scanning and typeahead work.
- **Numbers as numerals** ("3 sightings"), dates people can't misread ("28
  November 2026"), money with its currency where it's ambiguous.

## Labels, buttons and messages, by component

| Text | Write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Button | A verb and its object: what will happen | "Save address", "Log sighting", "Delete draft" |
| Link | Where it goes, readable on its own | "See all activity", not "Click here" |
| Field `label` | The thing being asked for, a noun | "Email", "Membership number" |
| `helperText` | Format, limits or why you're asking | "Starts with SQ, such as SQ-104-772" |
| Field `error` | What's wrong and how to fix it | "Enter a date in the past" |
| Fieldset `legend` | The question | "Which interests do you have?" |
| Heading | What the section or page is about | "Payment options" |
| Toast | What just happened, in the past tense | "Invite sent to Aroha" |
| Alert | What's going on, then what to do | "Your annual dues are due in 3 days. Pay by 28 November." |
| ConfirmModal | The question with its consequence; the action as the button | "Delete this draft? You can't undo this." with "Delete draft" |
| EmptyState | What would be here, and the next step | "No sightings yet" with "Log a sighting" |
| Badge | A state in one or two words | "Overdue", "3 new" |
| Switch label | The setting, not its state | "Email reminders" |
| Tooltip | A name or a few words, never instructions people need | "Search sightings" |

## Errors

- **Say what happened and what to do**, in that order. Don't blame the user.
- **Be specific**: "Enter an amount less than $10,000", not "Invalid value".
- **Don't say "error"** or show codes unless people need to quote them to
  support; then give the code after the plain words.
- **A failed load is not empty.** "We couldn't load your sightings. Try again."

## Consistent words

Pick one word for each thing and action and keep it: "Delete" or "Remove", not
both for the same thing; "Sighting report", not "Entry" on one screen and "Sighting report" on the
next. A button and the page it opens use the same words ("Add a bank account"
opens "Add a bank account").

## Accessibility

- Names must make sense out of context: screen readers list links, buttons and
  headings on their own.
- An icon-only control still needs words: IconButton's `label`.
- Say status in words as well as colour: a badge reads "Overdue", not only red.
- Avoid directions that depend on sight: "Select Save", not "Click the green
  button on the right".

## Pitfalls

- **Placeholders as labels or help.** They disappear when people type.
- **"Are you sure?"** Ask the real question with its consequence.
- **Marketing tone in a work screen.** "Awesome! You're all set!" adds nothing
  to "Sighting logged."
- **Words that change with state** on a control. A Switch's label stays the
  same whether it's on or off.
