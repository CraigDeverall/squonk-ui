# Multi-step forms

<ResizableCanvas of={Examples.MultiStep} defaultHeight={560} />

Split a long form when the steps are separate topics, or when a later step
depends on an earlier answer. Keep a single page when people need to see
everything together.

- **One form per step**, each with its own schema. A step can't be left until it
  is valid, and its error summary covers only that step.
- **Keep the answers in the parent**, so Back returns to filled-in fields.
- **Say where they are**: "Step 2 of 3", and the step's name as its heading.
- **Move focus to the new heading** on each step, so screen-reader users hear
  where they have arrived.
- **End with a check page** and send nothing until it is confirmed.

## One long page, or steps

| The form… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Is short, or people need to see every answer together | One page |
| Is long but people fill it in one sitting, in any order | One page with a heading (or a `Fieldset`) per topic, and the error summary at the top |
| Has separate topics, or later questions depend on earlier answers | Steps, as here |
| Is a quick task started from another page | A [Modal](?path=/docs/functional-modal--docs) or [Drawer](?path=/docs/functional-drawer--docs), only if it's a few fields; otherwise a page |

Save long forms as people go, or warn before they leave with unsaved answers.
The [Join the Society](?path=/story/examples-join-the-society--registration)
example is a long single-page form with sections and a review drawer.
